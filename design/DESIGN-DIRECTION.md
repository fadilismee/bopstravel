# Design direction

## Desired feel
Premium, calm, dependable transport — editorial spacing and confident typography, not a generic travel template. Original design language; no pixel-level imitation of the reference.

## Starting visual hypothesis
- Deep midnight/navy foundation with warm ivory surfaces and a restrained amber/copper accent.
- Large editorial headline, high-quality real fleet photography, generous whitespace.
- Strong contrast, clean grid, subtle borders and restrained motion.
- Mobile conversion CTA remains prominent without obscuring content.

## Components to design
Header/navigation, hero + route inquiry panel, service cards, route selector/list, fleet cards, trust/credentials block, process steps, FAQ accordion, footer, WhatsApp CTA, form validation and success/hand-off state.

## Design checks
Use actual imagery and approved logo before finalizing palette. Meet WCAG AA text contrast; support reduced motion; visible keyboard focus; tap targets around 44px; test 360px mobile, tablet, and desktop. Avoid invented badges, star ratings, customer counts, or safety claims.

## Content voice
Bahasa Indonesia, warm and direct. Explain what the visitor can do next. Distinguish inquiry from confirmed booking; avoid unsupported superlatives and guaranteed claims.
