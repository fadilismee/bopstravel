# Project brief

## Purpose
Prepare a premium, modern website for Armada Trans Java that helps prospective passengers understand services, discover routes and contact the operator confidently.

## Audience / jobs to be done
- Travelers arranging intercity transport from Jakarta and Bandung.
- Families or groups comparing private charter options.
- Visitors who need clear route coverage, vehicle choices, pickup expectations, and a fast way to ask for availability.

## Goals
1. Communicate services, coverage, and booking steps quickly.
2. Make mobile inquiry and WhatsApp handoff straightforward.
3. Improve trust through verified operational information, fleet imagery, policies, and FAQs.
4. Create scalable route pages with useful, non-duplicated content.

## Out of scope for initial release
- Reproducing the reference site's layout, copy, code, or images.
- Instant ticket issuance, payment processing, seat inventory, or guaranteed prices without a confirmed backend and operator workflow.
- Publishing unverified safety, licensing, insurance, schedule, or review claims.

## Success measures to define with owner
Qualified WhatsApp inquiries, route-page organic impressions, inquiry completion rate, mobile performance, and lead response time.

## Decisions / open questions
- Is this project authorized by Armada Trans Java, and who approves branding and content?
- Exact service types: scheduled regular travel, door-to-door, charter, tours, or a subset?
- Current routes, directionality, stops, schedules, days, fares, luggage rules, pickup boundaries, and availability process?
- Confirm official phone/WhatsApp, email, address, social profiles, and operating hours.
- Fleet inventory, capacities, amenities, accessibility, maintenance/safety evidence, and original approved photos?
- Booking/lead recipient, response SLA, privacy notice, and data retention?
- Preferred domain, hosting, CMS/editing workflow, analytics consent, SEO ownership?
- Languages and whether online payment / booking integration is planned?

## Guardrails
Treat public web listings as discovery only, not final truth. Obtain owner approval for all factual claims and rights-cleared assets. Show prices and schedules only with a source and update process. Collect the minimum personal data needed and disclose its use.
